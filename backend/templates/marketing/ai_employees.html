{# ── YOU DEFINE ── #}
You define

Every detail of how your AI employee works.

LiftBot gives you full control. Configure what your AI employee knows, how it behaves, and exactly when your team should step in.

01 · Role

What job does it perform?

Assign a specific business function — sales, support, reception or a custom role built around your exact process.

02 · Knowledge

What does it know?

Upload your products, services, FAQs, policies and any other business information your AI employee should use.

03 · Instructions

How should it behave?

Define the tone of voice, communication style and the specific behaviours your AI employee should follow.

04 · Goals

What should it help accomplish?

Set clear objectives — qualify leads, capture contact details, answer questions, or guide customers toward the next step.

05 · Rules

What should it avoid or escalate?

Set guardrails. Tell your AI employee what topics to avoid, what not to say, and what situations always need a human.

06 · Human Handoff

When should your team step in?

Define the exact conditions under which the conversation is handed to your team — with a full summary ready to go.
