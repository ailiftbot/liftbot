About LiftBot

We're building a new way for businesses to work with AI.

AI is becoming a bigger part of how businesses operate. But for many business owners, getting started still feels complicated.

{# ── ORIGIN STORY ── #}

"What if a business could simply hire an AI employee and give it a job?"

That's where LiftBot started.

There are countless AI tools, chatbots and automation platforms available. Yet the real question for us was much simpler than any of them.

We are building LiftBot to make AI more practical for everyday businesses — not just something impressive to experiment with, but something that can actually become part of the team.

So, what is LiftBot?

LiftBot is an AI employee platform that helps businesses create digital employees for specific roles.

An AI employee can be configured around a business's knowledge, instructions, responsibilities and way of communicating. It can help with things like:

Customer conversations Lead capture Lead qualification Customer support FAQs Initial enquiries Repetitive business tasks

Give AI a role. Give it the right knowledge. Let it help your team.

{# ── WHY WE STARTED ── #}
Why we started building it

The same problem, again and again.

We work closely with businesses and have seen it repeat itself. Businesses spend a lot of time answering the same questions, following up with enquiries, responding to customers and handling repetitive tasks — while customers expect a fast response.

We believe AI can help with this. But we don't think businesses need another complicated AI tool that requires a technical team to operate. We want to make the experience much simpler.

Create an AI employee. Train it around your business. Put it to work.

{# ── OUR APPROACH: AI + PEOPLE (reuses marketing.css split component) ── #}
Our approach to AI

AI + people. Not AI instead of people.

We don't believe AI should replace the people who make a business special. We believe it should support them.

Where AI helps
  • Conversations AI can handle very well
  • Repetitive, routine work
  • Initial questions and information requests
Where people are better
  • Customers, decisions and relationships
  • Conversations where a human will always be better
  • Parts of the business that need human judgment
{# ── BUILT FOR REAL BUSINESSES ── #}
Built for real businesses

Not built only in a lab.

We're testing LiftBot with real websites, real customer questions and real business scenarios — learning from what actually happens when customers interact with an AI employee, including where it works well, where it needs improvement, and where a human should step in.

Our goal isn't an AI employee that looks impressive in a demo. We want to build one that is genuinely useful.
