Our mission

Make every business always-on.

The businesses we work with are great at what they do — trades, professional services, healthcare, retail. What they're not always great at is being available 24 hours a day to answer the same questions, capture the same lead details, or route enquiries to the right person.

That's not a failure of the business. It's just the reality of running a team. LiftBot gives those businesses a digital team member who never needs a day off.

{# ── STORY / TIMELINE ── #}
Our journey

How we got here.

2022
The idea

Founder Arham noticed that the businesses he consulted for kept losing enquiries outside business hours. A simple idea: what if they had a team member who never logged off? or The business problem of losing after-hours customer enquiries sparked a simple solution: introducing a virtual team member who never logs off.

Early 2023
First prototype

We built a rough prototype and tested it with three local businesses. Within two weeks, each had captured leads they would otherwise have missed. We knew we were on to something.

Mid 2023
Brandinglift launches LiftBot

LiftBot becomes the flagship product of Brandinglift — a marketing technology company focused on helping UK businesses grow smarter. The first 20 customers go live.

2024
Multi-role AI employees

We expanded beyond a single AI chatbot to support multiple distinct employee roles — Sales, Support, Reception, Lead Qualification and Appointments — all configurable from one platform.

Today
Growing the AI workforce

LiftBot is live across dozens of UK businesses. We're continuing to build the platform, deepen integrations and explore what it means to give every business a full AI workforce.
